The third release candidate of Tidash 1.0: the interface polish and quick play. 1.0.0-rc.1 and 1.0.0-rc.2 installs update to it by themselves.

## New

- Play without opening first: hovering an album, playlist or mix card shows a play button in its cover's corner, and a playlist in the sidebar plays from its cover or its menu's Play. While that list plays, the button pauses it.
- Track rows play on a single click, in track lists and the queue.
- Links: in the player bar, the cover opens where playback started, the title the album and each artist that artist. In track lists and the queue, artist and album names open them.
- Toasts confirm what shows nothing else: Link copied, Playing next, Added to queue, favourites from a menu, and Add to playlist's answer.
- Pages and newly loaded covers fade in, buttons fade on hover, and nothing redraws at rest.
- Right-click menus are roomier, with an icon per item and separators between groups.
- The hand cursor shows on everything clickable, and plain text no longer selects.

## Fixed

- The seek and volume bars line up with their times and icons; their thumb shows on hover, focus or drag.
- The 960×540 minimum size holds when a tiling manager (GlazeWM, FancyZones) resizes the window.
- The sidebar toggle is the top bar's first control, every page ends with room above the player bar, and text fields, buttons and combo boxes are one height.
- On Linux with PipeWire, the output device list no longer shows other apps' playback streams.
- The decoder thread runs at a raised priority, so a busy machine is less likely to cause gaps.

## Linux

The builds now link `libpipewire-0.3.so.0`. The deb and rpm depend on it; with the tar.gz, install it if your system lacks it (desktops running PipeWire have it).

## Testing the update

With 1.0.0-rc.1 or 1.0.0-rc.2 installed (the Windows installer or zip, the macOS app in Applications, or the Linux tar.gz):

1. Open Settings › Updates and choose Check now, or wait for the check at start.
2. Tidash downloads 1.0.0-rc.3 in the background and shows Restart to update.
3. After the restart, Settings › Updates says Tidash 1.0.0-rc.3.

A deb, rpm or AppImage install does not update itself: Settings says so, and the new package is on this page.

## Performance (macOS, M2 Pro)

First frame after 367 ms, 80.1 MB on Home, no redraws and 0.0% of one core while idle, a 17.3 MB binary.
